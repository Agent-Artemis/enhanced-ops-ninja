How pricing works

Two parts, and which apply depends on how far up you go. Everything runs on a monthly subscription, priced per location with unlimited users — and you can pay monthly, pay annually for two months free, or own it outright with a single payment. The upper tiers also carry an up-front build cost, because connecting your systems or building something new is real work before it is a subscription. What you pay depends on how many locations you run, how many systems you want connected, and the scope of anything built for you. We scope it with you and put it in writing — no estimate on the first call.
