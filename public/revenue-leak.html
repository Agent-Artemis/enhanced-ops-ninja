The Revenue Leak Check

Where is your money quietly leaking?

Across multiple locations, revenue rarely disappears in one big loss — it drips out through denials, underpayments, and dips nobody catches until quarter-end. Four honest questions to find the leaks.

Question 1 · Billed vs. collected

Can you see, per location, how much you billed vs. actually collected last month — without pulling a special report?

Question 2 · Denied & underpaid claims

Do denied or underpaid claims get reworked automatically, or do some just quietly disappear?

Question 3 · Contracted rates

Do you know whether any payer is currently paying you below your contracted rate?

Question 4 · Early warning

Could you spot a location whose collections dropped this month before it becomes a quarter-end surprise?
