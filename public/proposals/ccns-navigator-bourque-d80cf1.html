02 What We're Building

An EMR-style workspace with a patient worklist: open a Veteran, get the checklist, and drive the pre-authorization from intake to submission to decision — with every call, document, and deadline logged against the case. Two workflow engines, one platform, folding into the EON EMR over time.

Pre-Authorization Engine Part One

The VA Community Care authorization workflow, systematized:
  • Case pipeline — intake → QA → submit → follow-up → decision → billing handoff
  • RFS packet builder — VA Form 10-10172, auto-filled, signature-ready, completeness-checked so incomplete packets can't go out
  • VAMC directory — the right fax number and contact per facility, the single most error-prone step, eliminated
  • 24 / 48 / 72-hour cadence engine — Barb's follow-up discipline enforced by software, not memory
  • Audit spine — every action, immutable, one-click case-file export

Credentialing Engine Part Two

Barb's other line of work, productized:
  • Provider & document repository keyed to NPI, with automatic text extraction from uploaded documents
  • Expirables & re-credentialing clocks — license, DEA, board cert, the 36-month cycle, CAQH's 120-day attestation
  • Payer-enrollment tracking across Medicare (PECOS), Medicaid, and VA CCN
  • Monthly exclusion monitoring (OIG / SAM / state) — now an NCQA requirement
  • The differentiator no incumbent has: automated phone follow-up to chase stalled enrollments

04 The Voice Layer — Positioned Honestly

The phones are a real cost: staff burn 15–25 minutes on hold per authorization call. A voice agent that dials, navigates the menu, sits through the queue, detects the human, and warm-transfers to your biller reclaims that time — that's the value, and it's buildable. What we will not pitch is a bot that autonomously "checks the auth and logs it" — nobody in this industry ships that without human review, and it would fail against real VA phone systems.

The more durable play is data, not dialing.

VA is actively pushing providers off the phone — much authorization status already lives in VA's portals. So the stronger long-term moat is pulling status automatically where a portal exists, with voice as the fallback for lines that only answer by phone. We build the reliable layer, not the demo.

05 Compliance Is a Feature, Not a Cost

The audit spine sells itself

Every case produces an immutable, exportable record linking the RFS to the authorization number to the claim. When a VA or Medicare audit lands, that file is answered in minutes. Given the $178.5M in mismatched-referral findings, this is what protects a provider — and what a compliance-minded client pays for.

Built clean from day one

HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance in place before any PHI ever flows. Append-only audit logging from the first commit.
