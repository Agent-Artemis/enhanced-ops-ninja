The ladder

One and two are the path. Three is where it goes for enterprise. Four sits alongside — it is not a rung.

1The two core productsStart here

Two systems, sold alone or together. This is where nearly every customer starts.

Ninja Operating System — $249/mo per location · unlimited users

Run your whole team: Goals, Scoreboard, Stand-ups, To-Dos, Org & SOPs, Training.

Survey Ninja — $249/mo per location · unlimited users

Your state's cited F-tags and Life Safety findings, ranked, with a prep workflow to work them.

Ninja Suite — both together — $399/mo per location · unlimited users

Both systems embedded in one site, so the team has a single login and one place to work. Sold as ease of use, not as analytics.

Who it is for

Any operator, single or multi-location, who needs the team running on one system. Survey Ninja lands hardest with anyone who has just been surveyed or is due.

What it does NOT include

No dashboard. No data visibility. The Suite puts both products in one site — it does not bring your data together or report on it. Say this plainly on the call. Promising a dashboard here is the single easiest way to lose the deal later.

Next step up: Command Center — when they ask “can I see it all in one view?”, that question is the upsell, and it is the one you are waiting for.
▼

2Command CenterThe upsell

“Bring all of your data to make smart decisions now. Put your data in the hands of the decision makers.”

$5,000 and up then $127 per connection, per location, per month

This is where the dashboard and the data actually live. Scales to enterprise.

Who it is for

Multi-location operators, and anyone whose decision makers are still waiting on someone to pull a report. The more systems they already run, the stronger this is.

What it does NOT include

Not custom software. It connects and surfaces what they already have — it does not build them something new. Each connection is priced separately, so scope the number of connections on the call rather than quoting a single monthly figure.

Next step up: Custom software buildout — when what they need does not exist yet.
▼

3Custom software buildoutEnterprise

Purpose-built software for a workflow no off-the-shelf product covers.

$25,000 and up per project · scoped individually
Who it is for

Enterprise, and operators with a workflow that is genuinely theirs. Usually arrives after Command Center, once the data is visible and the gap is obvious.

What it does NOT include

Never quote a price on the call. $25,000 is the floor, not an estimate. Scope, then quote — bring it back to the team before you put a number in writing.

Runs alongside the subscription tiers. It does not replace them.

4Marketing packageAdd-on · not a rung

Voice in multiple languages, SMS, chat management, coordination and scheduling for their sales team.

Priced per package sample: $1,800 / 100 hrs · plus data analytics charges
Who it is for

Anyone with inbound they are not answering fast enough, or a sales team losing leads to slow follow-up. Sells at any tier — it does not depend on the others.

What it does NOT include

The hours are the package; data analytics is charged on top. Quote both, or the first invoice will be a surprise. Confirm the languages they actually need before promising.

Attach at any rung — it is a parallel add-on, not the next step.

How to use this on a call

Lead with the problem, not the ladder. Ask what their team runs on today and what their last survey looked like. If they name a system they hate, that is tier 1. If they say “I can never get a straight answer out of the numbers”, that is tier 2 and you can go there in the same conversation.
Never promise the dashboard at $399. The Suite is one login and one site. The dashboard is Command Center. Getting this wrong is the fastest way to a refund conversation.
Say the unit every time you say a price. “$249 a month, per location, unlimited users.” A rep who drops the unit creates a pricing dispute at renewal.
