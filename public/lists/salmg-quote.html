SECTION 4

Two things stated plainly

QuickBooks Desktop

Desktop is materially harder than Online. The CRAWL phase includes a paid evaluation of middleware options and returns a go or no-go recommendation with costs before any Desktop integration is committed. No Desktop build price is quoted until that evaluation is delivered.

Competitor metrics

Competitor occupancy, competitor pricing and competitor promotions are not held in any system. Each metric in the Sales module will be marked automated, semi-automated or manual during Discovery, so you know which numbers arrive on their own and which take someone's time.

SECTION 5

Ownership

SAL owns the resulting system as a company asset that transfers with the business. EnhancedOps.Ninja retains its underlying platform and methodology, and serves as SAL's exclusive provider for hosting, operation and support. Full terms are in the Service Agreement.

SECTION 6

What unlocks the timeline
