Why owners actually do this

Software you own doesn't just cut costs. It re-rates the business.

Veterinary is consolidating fast, and consolidators pay on a multiple of EBITDA. A group that runs on someone else's software sells as a services business. A group that owns its own operating software — built around its workflows, with the data to prove it — sells as something else.

1–2x on the multiple

Owned software and the IP behind it is the difference buyers pay up for. A $20M group at 3x is $60M — with owned software in the deal, closer to $80M.

Priced to match what you already pay

This is designed to land at or under your current software spend — not on top of it. You're redirecting a bill you already pay into an asset you keep.

Clean data at diligence

When a buyer asks for three years of per-clinic performance, it's one export — not six systems and a quarter of cleanup.
